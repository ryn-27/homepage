About me

I’m Ryne Angelo Rigor, a Bachelor of Multimedia Arts student who draws portraits.

I started with traditional media, mainly pencil and ink, and I’ve spent most of my practice learning how to capture a person’s character in a face.

Lately I’ve been learning digital illustration to widen what I can create. My goal is to grow into a professional illustrator.
